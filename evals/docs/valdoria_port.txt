The Port of Valdoria

The Port of Valdoria is a harbor town on the northern coast of the fictional Estmark peninsula. It was founded in 1874 by the merchant Henrik Aldane, who built the first wooden pier to ship salted fish. Today the town has a population of 18,400.

Harbor and piers

The harbor has a depth of 14 meters at high tide, which allows mid-sized freighters to dock. It has three piers: North Pier, Lantern Pier, and Saltgate. Lantern Pier is reserved for passenger ferries. The harbor master since 2011 is Ingrid Voss, who introduced the colored-flag system for berth assignments.

Trade

The port's main export is dried amber kelp, which is sold to cosmetics makers abroad. Kelp is harvested by hand from boats between April and October. The port also exports small quantities of smoked eel, but this accounts for less than five percent of revenue.

Lighthouse and ferry

The Valdoria lighthouse was built in 1902 and stands 42 meters tall. Its lamp was converted from oil to electricity in 1958. A ferry connects the port to Ostrel Island and runs twice daily, leaving at 8:15 and 17:40.

Culture

Every year on the first weekend of September the town holds the Kelp Festival, which includes a rowing race and a market of handmade rope goods.
