Hollow Creek Apiary: Keeper's Notes

Hollow Creek Apiary was started by Marta Quillon and currently keeps 14 hives on a south-facing hillside. All hives use 10-frame Langstroth boxes painted pale green.

Inspections and queens

During summer the hives are inspected every 9 days. Swarm season peaks in May, so inspections are done more often then, with a focus on finding queen cells. Queens are marked with a colored dot according to the year; the 2024 queens are marked blue.

Harvest

The main honey harvest takes place in late August. In the most recent season the harvest averaged 31 kg of honey per hive. Honey is extracted with a hand-cranked extractor and stored in food-grade buckets for two weeks before jarring.

Winter care

In autumn the bees are fed sugar syrup mixed at a ratio of 2 parts sugar to 1 part water. Varroa mites are treated with oxalic acid in December, when the colonies have little or no brood. Entrance reducers are installed in November to keep mice out.
