# Meridian-7 Solid-State Cell

## Overview

The Meridian-7 is a solid-state battery cell developed by Calder Labs and first released in 2023. It uses a ceramic-polymer composite electrolyte and a nickel-manganese cathode.

## Performance

The cell reaches an energy density of 410 Wh/kg. It can be charged to 80 percent in 18 minutes. Its rated cycle life is 2,500 full cycles before capacity drops below 80 percent. The operating temperature range is from -30 to 60 degrees Celsius.

## Safety

In laboratory abuse tests, no thermal runaway was observed at temperatures up to 150 degrees Celsius. The cell has an integrated pressure sensor that signals the battery management system if swelling is detected.

## Production

The cells are manufactured at the Tamsin factory, which produces 40,000 cells per month. In 2024, batch 12 was recalled after a fault was found in the pressure sensor firmware. The recall affected about 3,000 cells, and affected customers received replacements free of charge.

## Warranty

Calder Labs offers a warranty of 8 years on the Meridian-7, or 2,000 cycles, whichever comes first.
